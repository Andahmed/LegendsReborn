# Style Guide

## Approved direction
- Stylized 3D.
- Palette relationship: turquoise × gold × sand/earth.
- Arabic geometric ornament in architecture, props, textiles and UI framing; readable, not cluttered.
- Strong mobile-distance hero silhouettes, distinctive dominant colors, animation language and voice/audio identity.
- Combat VFX must communicate hit, heal, control, faction advantage and Ultimate without obscuring the 5v5 field.

## Production constraints
- Art, animation, VFX and audio are critical path; target approximately one-third of production capacity.
- Prove the pipeline with four representative heroes and one complete environment before scaling to 20 heroes and five environments.
- Optimize meshes, materials, textures, rigs and overdraw for target low-end Android.
- Track source and license for every imported asset; use original or properly licensed assets only.

## Acceptance checklist
- [ ] Three mood directions documented and one approved.
- [ ] Palette tokens and UIStyle values approved.
- [ ] Hero proportions, rig, polycount, texture and animation budgets approved.
- [ ] One complete hero (model, rig, animations, VFX, audio) integrated into a playable battle.
- [ ] One complete environment integrated into a playable campaign scene.
- [ ] Performance verified on target Android device.
- [ ] Voice/audio identity plan approved for the 20 MVP heroes.
