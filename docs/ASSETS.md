## Model and texture budgets

| Type | Maximum triangles | Required scale / format | Texture rule |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Hero | 8,000 | 1 Unity unit = 1 metre; height 1.6–2.0 m; feet pivot; front +Z; Unity-generated Humanoid rig | Flat-color materials; no atlas required |
| Enemy | 6,000 | Same scale, pivot and forward direction | Flat-color materials |
| Prop | 1,500 | FBX exported from Unity-generated Mesh asset or prefab | Flat-color materials |
| Complete battle environment | 40,000 | FBX exported from Unity-generated Mesh asset or prefab | Flat-color materials |
| VFX | Particle System prefab | Assets/Art/VFX/<faction>/ | No large texture dependencies |

The four hero meshes are built to 1.9 m height with a feet-level origin and +Z front, then exported as FBX by Unity FBX Exporter 5.1.1. Hero and enemy prefabs contain a Humanoid Avatar and the seven named animation clips: Idle, Run, Attack, Skill, Ultimate, Hit and Death.

Portrait PNGs are 128x128. Android texture importer settings are ASTC 6x6. No 4K textures are generated.

## Required output locations

- `Assets/Art/Characters/<hero_id>/hero_<id>.fbx`: exported humanoid model.
- `Assets/Art/Characters/<hero_id>/meshes/`: generated source mesh assets.
- `Assets/Art/Characters/<hero_id>/anims/`: seven animation clips.
- `Assets/Art/Characters/<hero_id>/`: Humanoid Avatar and Animator Controller.
- `Assets/Art/Characters/Enemies/gloom_raider/enemy_gloom_raider.fbx`: exported enemy model.
- `Assets/Art/Characters/Enemies/gloom_raider/`: enemy mesh, avatar, animations and prefab.
- `Assets/Art/Environments/<biome>/env_<biome>.fbx`, environment prefab and `props/` FBX files.
- `Assets/Art/VFX/<faction>/`: skill and ultimate Particle System prefabs.
- `Assets/Prefabs/Heroes/`: four battle-ready hero prefabs.
- `Assets/Prefabs/UI/`: portraits and frame sprites.
- `Assets/Data/Stages/Stage_001.asset`: stage enemy and environment prefab references.

No generated or unused asset is placed under `Resources/`. HeroDefinition references the hero prefab directly; StageDefinition references enemy and environment prefabs directly.

## Generation and acceptance

Run `Sands > Setup Prototype` in Unity 2022.3.62f3 to generate the registered assets and connect them to Battle. Run `Sands > Validate Assets` to check the asset register, triangle limits, Humanoid avatars, seven clips, stage references, Resources restrictions and Android ASTC settings. The 3D work is not accepted until the generated characters are visible in the Battle scene and the scene has been playtested.
