# GDD (condensed) - Sands of Legends

Source: [Idle_RPG_Project_Plan.pdf](Idle_RPG_Project_Plan.pdf). Read it fully; this is the short version.

## Core loop
Campaign stage -> auto battle (60-90s at x1) -> rewards (gold/exp/shards/gear mats) -> upgrade heroes -> harder stage.
Idle rewards accumulate up to 12h offline. Energy (cap 120, +1/6min) is spent only on farming/events, never on the normal campaign.
Session target: 10-15 min, 4+ sessions/day. Day-1 should feel full without being exhausting.

## Heroes
- 20 heroes for MVP, 4 rarities: Common / Rare / Epic / Legendary (test pull rates 40/35/20/5).
- Pity: guaranteed Epic every 30 pulls, Legendary every 90. Duplicates -> shards -> stars (5 levels).
- Roles: Tank / Support / DPS. Every faction has at least one tank, one support, one DPS.
- Levels: shared "Spirit Source" ceiling; a new hero is raised to (ceiling - 5) immediately.
- Gear: 6 slots, sets, upgraded with gear materials.

## Factions
Fortress > Wild > Forest > Shadow > Fortress (+25% damage). Divinity <-> Abyss rare, mutual advantage.
Team synergy: 3+2 factions = +15% HP; single faction = +25% ATK/HP (numbers tunable).

## Combat
5-slot formation: 2 front + 3 back. Auto skills with timing; Ultimate when energy is full (auto or manual). Speeds x1/x2/x4.
Depth comes from pre-battle decisions (team/roles/faction counters), not from execution.

## Modes (MVP vs after launch)
MVP: Campaign (200 stages, 5 biomes x 40), Idle farming, Weekly boss, PvP arena (async), daily quests.
After launch: Endless tower, guilds, seasonal events, Battle Pass.

## Design pillars
1. Always visible progress, even when away. 2. A meaningful decision every ~10 minutes.
3. Heroes with identity (written characters, own voice). 4. Respect player time (x2/x4, daily quests in minutes).
5. Fair F2P: free path reaches endgame; purchases speed up, never gate.

## Legal / IP
Mechanics are not protected, but names, characters, art, audio, text and UI are. Never copy from Legends Reborn: Last Battle / Magnum Quest.
