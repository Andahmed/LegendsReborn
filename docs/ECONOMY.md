# Economy

## Five resources
| Resource | Role | Required source/sink definition |
|---|---|---|
| Gems | Premium currency | Earned quests/events/approved ads and purchases; summons and approved premium sinks |
| Gold | General progression | Campaign/idle/activities; hero and gear upgrades |
| Experience | Hero levels | Campaign, dedicated farms, quests; level cap governed by Spirit Source |
| Hero Shards | Star progression | Duplicate conversion, events, approved shops |
| Equipment Materials | Gear progression | Dedicated farms, weekly boss, events; equipment upgrades |

Each resource needs a ledger: sources, sinks, caps, daily expected income/spend and progression by stage. Day 1–14 ledger is not complete.

## Five monetization channels
1. Summon banners.
2. Monthly card.
3. Battle Pass.
4. Voluntary/rewarded ads.
5. Tiered offers.

## Safeguards
- F2P players must be able to reach endgame; spending accelerates but does not gate mandatory power.
- Publish official summon rates and pity.
- Summon RNG/results are server-authoritative and auditable.
- Do not invent prices, rewards, ad frequency, offer values or progression curves. Tune only against approved specifications and soft-launch evidence.
